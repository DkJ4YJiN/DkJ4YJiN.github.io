Use this page offline离线使用本页

Open this page once before you fly. Then save a copy. A saved copy opens with no network, even when the link is blocked in China.出发前请先打开本页一次，然后保存一份副本。副本无需网络即可打开，即使链接在中国被屏蔽也能用。

  • iPhone (Safari): tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, or Save to Files.iPhone（Safari）：点分享，再点“添加到主屏幕”或“存储到文件”。
  • Android (Chrome): open the menu, then Add to Home screen, or Download.安卓（Chrome）：打开菜单，点“添加到主屏幕”或“下载”。
